Trap Runner — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 June 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the mobile game Trap Runner (the “App”), published by Dalvoy, handles information.

Data we collect

We do not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. Trap Runner does not require an account, does not include analytics, and does not connect to any server.

Data stored on your device

The App saves your game progress and settings (such as unlocked levels, selected character, sound preferences, and total deaths) locally on your device using standard app storage. This information never leaves your device and is removed if you uninstall the App.

Permissions

The App does not request access to your contacts, location, camera, microphone, photos, or files.

Children

The App is suitable for general audiences and does not knowingly collect any information from children.

Changes

If future versions add features such as advertising or in-app purchases, this policy will be updated to describe any data those features process.
